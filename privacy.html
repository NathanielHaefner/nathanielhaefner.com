What I Collect

The only information I collect is the data that you provide to me by using my contact page. I do not collect analytics data, nor do I use scripts to track you or your activity on my site. My contact form is entirely up to you to use or avoid. You also don’t have to use any real information in the contact, but you risk me not being able to respond to your questions.

If you choose to send me a message or a doodle using my contact page, I will have whatever you type in the name field, email field, and message field. The doodle is optional.

By sending me a doodle, I will have an email with your doodle, as well as a copy of your doodle in a database on my server. The doodle is a simple image with a filename generated using your name and the date & time you submitted the form.

Regarding Minors

In order to use the contact form, you must be 14 years or older.

I don’t collect any information that you don’t actively give me!

—Nathaniel Haefner

How I Use Your Information

I only use the information you provide me to get in touch with you at your request. If you submit a doodle, I might look at the doodle. The doodle has the name you gave in the input field and the date & time, which just helps me keep them organized in a database.

Sharing Information

I don’t share it. Consider it confidential.

Ads, Analytics, & Cookies

I don’t show ads or use any analytics tools. I also do not use cookies to store any information in your browser. The site does use a simple session storage value to remember if you want to see the site’s grid or use dark mode as you move from page to page. Session values are local to your computer and are erased as soon as you close your browser or the specific tab you used to view my site.

However, A few of my pages have embedded YouTube videos, which use only essential cookies important for proper functionality. The YouTube videos are embedded with "Privacy Enhanced Mode" which will not use tracking cookies or ad-based cookies. These videos are GDPR compliant.

Your Choices

If you submit a message (and doodle) through my contact page, I will receive an email with your name (the one you entered), email address (the one you entered), your message and doodle. If you do not want me to have this information, do not send me a message using the contact form.

If you have already contacted me and you wish for me to delete your information (and doodle), contact me again with a request to delete it in your message. I will gladly remove all traces of you from my email and doodle database.

Contact Me

If you would rather contact me directly through email about anything regarding my work or any data obtained by this site, please email me at nate@nathanielhaefner.com
