Font Focus

Font Focus aims to find links between types of fonts used, presentation of fonts, and font technology to help students read faster and retain more of the information they read.

This page is the testing page. Here you can set the parameters of the text including font style and size to test which of those parameters affects your ability to read text quickly and accurately.

You may also choose between three different reading modes: Traditional, Underline, and Focus to see which one helps increase your reading speed and comprehension.

After choosing a passage to read, there will be a short, five question quiz about what you have read to check for comprehension.
